R was used to create Multiple Linear Regression Models that examined various factors that relate to breastfeeding self-efficacy, such as the source of breastfeeding problems, external social support, and tools used by the mother. Alongside the results of Targeted Maximum Likelihood Estimations performed by a team member on this project, this work found that the number and types of problems experienced during breastfeeding may be a strong indicator of breast-feeding self-efficacy which can aid in adjusting healthcare programming related to mothers.
